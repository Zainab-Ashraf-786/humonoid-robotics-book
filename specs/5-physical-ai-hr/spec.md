## Key Entities
- **Physical AI**: Field combining AI and robotics to create embodied systems that learn from physical interaction
- **Embodied Intelligence**: Intelligence that emerges from interaction with the physical environment
- **Humanoid Robotics**: Robots with human-like structure and capabilities
- **LLM Integration**: Large Language Model integration for cognitive planning
- **Simulation-to-Reality Transfer**: Techniques for transferring skills learned in simulation to physical robots
- **ROS 2 Integration**: Robot Operating System communication and coordination
- **NVIDIA Isaac**: AI framework for perception and navigation
- **Unity/Gazebo**: Simulation environments from Module 2
- **VLA (Vision-Language-Action)**: Framework combining perception, cognition, and action
- **Cognitive Planning**: High-level reasoning and decision making with LLMs
- **Human-Robot Interaction**: Natural interaction between humans and humanoid robots
